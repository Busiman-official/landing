Open **Purchases** to buy from suppliers and receive goods. You need the Purchases module.

The tabs are **Receipts**, **Orders**, **Suppliers** and **Returns**.

## Receipts

Use Receipts to record goods that have arrived.

1. Choose the **receipt date** and the **receiving location**.
2. If the goods belong to an open purchase order, link it (press **Ctrl+Shift+O**).
3. Add what was received.
4. Post the receipt, or keep it as a draft.

Busiman keeps up to 12 held drafts for each branch and location on this computer.

## Orders (purchase orders)

- See the list with totals and filters.
- Create an order.
- Open an order to **confirm** it, **cancel** it or **receive goods** against it.

## Suppliers

See, add and open suppliers. A supplier's page shows their profile, purchase orders, bills and payment history. From here you can record a supplier payment.

## Returns

Send goods back to a supplier by starting a return from their bills. A return can be posted. Open a return to see its details and settlement options (debit note, refund or replacement).
