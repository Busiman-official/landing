## Everyday tips

- **Keep Busiman running.** Closing the window just hides it. That is what lets attendance work in the background.
- **Check you are checked in.** Do not assume a quiet start means success. Look at your status (see [Attendance](/docs/desktop-app/attendance)).
- **Check out before leaving.** Do not rely on the sleep or shutdown question appearing.
- **Use Ctrl+K** to find things quickly (see [Quick reference](/docs/desktop-app/quick-reference)).
- **Watch the red markers.** For example, on Sales points they show a counter is missing.
- **Held orders and drafts stay on the computer** until you finish or discard them.
- **Busiman works online.** If you see the banner "You are offline", your session is kept, but work you try to do will not go through. Nothing is saved and sent later. Wait until you are connected and do it again.

## If you share a computer

Busiman is designed for one person per computer account. If several people use the same computer, take these steps:

1. **Only Admins can sign out.** Other roles have no Logout item. If you hand over a computer to someone else, ask an Admin to sign you out (or to use Logout on your account from Workforce), or use a separate computer user account on the PC for each person.
2. **Held orders are shared.** Held and in-progress sales are saved per branch and sales point, not per person. The next person using that counter can see them. Finish or discard them, and do not leave customer details in held orders.
3. **A saved check-out can be offered to the next person.** If someone chose **Continue** at a sleep, lock or shutdown question, the next start may offer a check-out at that earlier time to whoever signs in. Choose **Skip** if the time is not yours.
4. **Busiman starts with the computer.** It reopens with the last signed-in person's session. Do not leave it signed in on a computer others can use.
5. **Lock your computer** when you step away.
