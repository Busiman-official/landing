> Busiman Desktop is the Windows and macOS app that your team uses every day for attendance, stock, sales at the counter, purchases, service jobs and the calendar. This guide explains how it works from your first sign-in to everyday tasks, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for business owners, managers and staff. You do not need any technical knowledge. Each chapter says which roles can use a feature, so you can skip what does not apply to you.

You will not see every feature described here. What you see depends on your **role** (for example Admin, HR, Manager or Employee) and on which **modules** (parts of Busiman) your branch has switched on.
