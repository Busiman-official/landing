## Signing in and your session

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "No account found..." | The number is not registered for this company. | Check the number, or ask your Admin or HR. |
| "This account isn't active..." | Your account is switched off. | Contact your Admin. |
| "Incorrect password." | Wrong password. | Retry, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| "Too many attempts. Please wait a bit and try again." | Too many wrong tries. | Wait a while, then retry. |
| "No connection to the server. Check your network and try again." | No internet or the server cannot be reached. | Check your connection. |
| "That code didn't work. Please try again." or "Enter the 4-digit code." | The SMS code was wrong or missing. | Re-type it, or use **Resend code** after the countdown. |
| "Password must be at least 8 characters." / "Passwords do not match." | New password problem on the reset screen. | Fix and retry. |
| You are suddenly back at the sign-in screen with no message | Your session ended (for example an Admin signed you out or reset your password). | Sign in again. Unsaved form entries are lost. Held sales on this computer are kept. |
| "Your session has expired. Please log in again." | Same as above. | Sign in again. |
| Blank area after signing in (Inventory Approver) | This role has no dashboard. | Use the top bar, for example **Inventory**. |

## Attendance

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Attendance Marked - You have been automatically checked in" | Automatic check-in worked. | Nothing. |
| "Check-In Failed" with a reason | The server refused (for example too early, too late, no shift, holiday, not scheduled today, network not approved). | Read the reason. Ask HR if it looks wrong. |
| "Network Error" | The check-in could not reach the server. | Check your connection. Check in again if you are an Employee. |
| "Unable to mark attendance. Please try again or contact support." | Something unexpected happened. | Try again, or contact support. |
| No message at all and you are not checked in | Busiman could not check you in and does not always say so. | Check your status as described in [Attendance](/docs/desktop-app/attendance). |
| "Network connection is not approved for attendance" | Your network is not on the approved list. | Join the office network, or ask HR to add it. |
| "No network connection detected. Please connect to a WiFi or Ethernet network." | Busiman found no Wi-Fi or Ethernet. | Connect and use the refresh button. |
| "Network connection validation failed. Please check your connection." | Busiman could not check your network. | Retry. |
| "This device has already been used by another employee today." | Shown when marking attendance from a device already used by someone else today. | Contact HR or Admin. |
| "Manual check-in is not allowed for this employee's shift. Please contact the administrator to enable manual check-ins for this shift." | The person's shift does not allow manual check-in (shown to non-Admins). | Ask an Admin or HR to change the shift rule, or have an Admin mark it. |
| "Employee not found. Please refresh the page and try again." | The person could not be found. | Refresh the list. |
| "Server error. Please try again later." | A problem on the server. | Wait and retry. |
| "Check-out Failed" / "The system will shut down anyway. You may need to manually check out later." | The check-out could not be sent. | Check out again as soon as you are back online. |

## Sales and purchases

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "This counter session is closed. You can't make a sale from this sales point." | The counter has no open session. | **Sales > Sales points > Open session**. |
| "...You can't save an order from this sales point." | Same, when saving an order. | Open the session. |
| "...Open a session to create a quotation." | Same, for a quotation. | Open the session. |
| "Add items before holding" | Cart is empty. | Add products first. |
| "Unknown barcode." | The code is not in your products. | Search by name, or check the barcode. |
| "Select at least one line with a return quantity." | Nothing was chosen in a return. | Enter a quantity on at least one line. |
| "Return failed" | The return was not saved. | Try again, or ask your Admin. |
| Charge is greyed out | See "When Charge is greyed out" in [Sales](/docs/desktop-app/sales). | Fix the cause. |

## General messages

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "You do not have permission to perform this action." | Your role cannot do this. | Ask your Admin. |
| "The requested resource was not found." | The item no longer exists. | Refresh. |
| "Server error. Please try again later." | Problem on the server. | Wait and try again. |
| "Unable to connect to the server. Please check your internet connection." | You are offline. | Check your connection. |
| "The request took too long. Please try again." | Slow connection. | Retry. |
| "You are offline. Your session is kept until you sign out..." banner | Computer has no internet. | Reconnect. Actions are not queued, so repeat anything that did not go through. |
| "Application Error - Could not load the application files. Please reinstall the application." | The app files are damaged or missing. | Reinstall Busiman. |

## Devices

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "You do not have permission to act as a proxy server." | You lack proxy permission. | Ask your Admin if you need it. |
| "Port 3002 is already in use" | Another program uses it. | Close that program, or ask IT. |
| "Proxy server is running but not registered with the main server..." | This computer could not register. | Check your connection, or ask IT. |
