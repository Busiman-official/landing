These items are visible or mentioned in the app, but they do not do anything yet. Do not rely on them.

- **Submitting attendance from outside the office** (remote attendance request). The desktop app has no form for it. The Approvals tab only handles requests that arrive from elsewhere.
- **Calendar Timeline view.** Only Day, Week and Month are available.
- **Logs viewer** in Settings.
- **Notifications** and **Attendance Reminders** in Settings > Attendance.
- **Preferences** in Inventory > Settings.
- **Batch selection** in the point of sale (the cart shows "Batch: coming soon.") and the **QR code** in serial number details.
- **Leave requests, payroll, recruitment, performance reviews and reports** shown as cards on dashboards. These are descriptions only.
- **Expense module** screens on desktop.
- **Logout for roles other than Admin.**
- **A dashboard for the Inventory Approver role.**
