Open **Service** to manage repair or service jobs for customers. You need the Service module. Jobs are created elsewhere (for example at reception or in the mobile app). The desktop app is for viewing and managing them.

## Find a job

The list can be filtered by status: Created, Assigned, Scheduled, Kit Ready, In Progress, Pending, Completed, Closed or Cancelled. You can search by customer, job number or serial number.

## Open a job

Select a job to open its panel, titled "Job #(number)". It shows the customer, type (On-site or In-office), the issue, where it came from, a timeline and the materials issued.

## Reassign, close or cancel

- **Reassign** gives the job to another technician. Remaining unused kit stock moves to the new technician automatically.
- **Close job** is available once a job is Completed. Busiman asks "Close this job? Confirm billing/inventory are reconciled first."
- **Cancel job** needs a reason from the list, then asks "Cancel this job? This cannot be undone."
