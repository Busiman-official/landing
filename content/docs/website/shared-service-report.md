This part is for **customers**. A business that repaired or serviced something for you may send you a link to your service report. It may arrive by message or email.

## How it works

- Tap or click the link. The report opens in your browser. You do not need to log in or install anything.
- **Anyone who has the link can open the report.** Share it only with people you trust.
- The report is not listed on the website, and search engines are told to stay away from it.
- The page has no top bar or footer.

> **Keep your link private.** The report may show your name, machine details, amounts and the technician's name. A preview of the link in a chat app can also show some of this.

## What you see

The report looks like a single white sheet. From top to bottom:

1. **The business details:** the company logo (if it has one), the company name, and a line with the branch name, address and phone.
2. **Report number and date:** on the right, shown as "#" and the number, with the completion date below. If there is no date, you see "—".
3. **Service Report:** the heading.
4. **Under the heading:** **Customer** (your name). **Technician** and **Device Serial** appear only when the business filled them in.
5. **Machines Serviced:** a table with **Item**, **Serial** and **Issue**. A dash "—" means there is no serial number.
6. **Parts Used:** a table with **Part** and **Qty**.
7. **Parts Removed:** a table with **Part** and **Qty**.
8. **Payment Summary:** **Parts**, **Service Charge** and **Amount Received**. A **Pending** line shows only when money is still due. Amounts are in Indian rupees, for example ₹1,250.00.
9. **Download PDF:** a button at the bottom.

Sections with nothing in them are left out. So you may not see every section.

## On a phone

The report uses the same layout on a phone. The tables can look tight on a narrow screen, and long text wraps inside the cells. You can zoom in to read.

## Save the report as a PDF

1. Tap or click **Download PDF** at the bottom of the report.
2. Your browser opens its print window.
3. Choose **Save as PDF** as the destination. Some phone browsers word this differently.
4. In the print window, **turn off "Headers and footers"** if your browser offers that option. This stops the link from being printed on the PDF.
5. Save the file.

The **Download PDF** button does not make a file by itself. It opens your browser's print window, and the website does not store the PDF. The button is not part of the saved page.

If the print window does not open, open the link in your phone's or computer's main browser (for example, use "Open in browser" from the chat app), then tap **Download PDF** again.

## If the link stops working

How long a link keeps working is up to the business that shared it. If a link stops working, you will see a plain page that says the page could not be found. Ask the business for a new link.
