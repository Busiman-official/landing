## The header (top bar)

The top bar stays at the top as you scroll. It looks different on a phone than on a computer.

| Where you are | What the top bar shows |
|---|---|
| Wider screens (computers, and some tablets held sideways) | The Busiman logo, **Features**, **Call: +91 7500673358** and a **Download** button |
| Narrower screens (phones and some other small screens) | Only the Busiman logo and **Call: +91 7500673358** |

There is no menu button on phones. On a narrower screen, the header does not show **Features** or **Download**. Use these instead:

- To download: tap **Download** in the main banner of the home page, or scroll down to the **Download** section.
- To see features: scroll to the Features part of the home page and tap **See the full feature deep dive →**, or use **Features** in the footer.

The Busiman logo takes you back to the home page from any page.

## The footer (bottom of the page)

The footer shows "© 2026 Busiman. Free office management for every business." It has these links:

| Link | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| **Features** | The Features page |
| **Docs** | The Documentation landing page |
| **Blog** | The list of blog posts |
| **Privacy Policy** | The Privacy Policy |
| **Terms** | The Terms of Service |
| **Contact** | The Contact section on the home page |

The footer is the easiest way to move around on a phone.

> **Note:** The footer does not link to "Delete your account". See [Deleting Your Account](/docs/website/legal-and-account-deletion#deleting-your-account) for its address.

## Documentation pages look different

Documentation pages have their own top bar instead of the main header and footer. It has the logo (goes to the home page), **Docs** (goes to the Documentation landing page) and **← Back to site** (goes to the home page). See [Documentation](/docs/website/documentation).

## Shared service reports

A shared service report page has no header or footer. See [Opening a Shared Service Report](/docs/website/shared-service-report).

## The tab icon

The site has a small Busiman icon that shows on your browser tab.
