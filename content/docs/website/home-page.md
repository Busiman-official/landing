## The first-visit splash screen

The first time you open the home page in a browser tab, a full-screen Busiman logo animation appears. It shows for about one second, then fades out over half a second. After that, the page appears.

- It appears only on the home page.
- You do not need to tap or press anything. There is also no way to skip it.
- It shows once per browser tab. If you open the home page again in the same tab, you skip it. A new tab shows it again.
- Your page scrolling is locked for that brief moment.

If you see the splash on a repeat visit, it may flash briefly before it disappears. This is normal.
