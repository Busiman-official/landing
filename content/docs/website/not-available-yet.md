---
title: "Not Available Yet on the Website"
description: "Things you might expect on the Busiman website that are not available yet, such as an iOS app, search and a contact form."
status: "live"
platform: "both"
---

- **iOS app:** marked **Soon** on the Download section. You cannot download it now.
- **A step-by-step page for every single feature:** the Desktop App Guide and Mobile App Guide explain each area chapter by chapter, but only Getting Started and Attendance have their own detailed, feature-by-feature pages.
- **Sales and Purchase on mobile:** the Documentation says these are desktop-only for now.
- **Search:** the website and the Documentation have no search box.
- **Contact form or chat:** none. Use phone or email.
- **A button to delete your account:** none. Use the email steps above.
- **Login on the website:** none. Busiman's login is inside the app.
