## The Features page

Open it from **Features** in the header (on computers), **Features** in the footer, or **See the full feature deep dive →** on the home page. The page title is "Features: Calls, Attendance, Inventory & Sales". Its heading is "One free app, built out of real modules".

Features are grouped into three categories.

| Category | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Field & customer ops** | Call Management; Field Service Management (FSM), tagged **Flagship module**; Attendance & Shifts |
| **Stock & money** | Sales; Purchase; Inventory Management; Expenses |
| **Team & business** | Employees & Teams; Multi-Branch & Company; Notifications & Alerts |

At the bottom is a band called "Every module, zero cost" with two buttons: **Download** and **Contact Us**.

> **About the numbers:** The home page says "Six modules" and the Features page lists more items. Treat the count as a rough idea of what is included, not an exact total.

## The Field Service Management page

Field Service Management (FSM) covers work done outside the office, such as repair visits.

To open it, go to the Features page and tap **See the full breakdown →** on the FSM card. The page is titled "Free Field Service Management (FSM) Software". Its heading is "Get every field job done right, the first time."

On this page you find:

- **Download** and **Contact Us** buttons
- Three short promises: **Free forever**, **Works on desktop & mobile** and **No per-engineer pricing**
- A picture of a job board. Its jobs are samples only.
- An explanation: "What is Field Service Management?"
- "Everything a field team needs, connected"
- "Any business whose work leaves the office"
- "FSM doesn't work alone", which shows how FSM connects with other modules
- A "Frequently asked" section
- A closing band, "Put field service on one board", with **Download**, **Contact Us** and **← Back to all features**
