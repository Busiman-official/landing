---
title: "Website Glossary"
description: "Plain-language meanings of the terms used on the Busiman website and in this guide."
status: "live"
platform: "both"
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Badge** | A small label on a documentation page, such as **Available now** or **Partially available** |
| **Browse docs** | The button on a phone that opens the list of documentation pages |
| **Code-signed** | A digital stamp that tells Windows who made an app. The Windows app does not have one yet. |
| **Desktop** | A computer app for Windows or macOS |
| **FSM (Field Service Management)** | Tools for work done outside the office, such as repair visits |
| **Mobile** | The phone app (Android now, iOS soon) |
| **Module** | One part of Busiman, such as Attendance or Sales |
| **On this page** | A list of headings on a page that you can tap to jump to |
| **Print window** | The window your browser opens when you print or choose **Save as PDF** |
| **Service report** | A record of a repair or service job, which a business can share with its customer |
| **Shared link** | An unlisted web address that opens one service report. Anyone who has it can open the report. |
| **Splash screen** | The brief full-screen logo animation the first time you open the home page in a tab |
