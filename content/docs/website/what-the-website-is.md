The Busiman website is the public home of **Busiman**, a free app that helps a business manage its office. The app covers calls, attendance, inventory, sales, purchase and service reports.

On the website you can:

- Learn what Busiman does and who it is for
- Download the app for Windows, macOS or Android
- Read setup guides (Documentation) and practical business articles (Blog)
- Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
- Call or email the Busiman team
- Open a service report that a business has shared with you by link

You do not need an account to use the website. It has no login, no sign-up form and no contact form.

> **Good to know:** The website is in English. The Busiman team can help you in Hindi or English by phone or email.

## Quick Start

1. Open **busiman.org** in your browser.
2. Tap or click **Download** in the main banner at the top of the home page.
3. Choose **Windows**, **macOS** or **Android**.
4. After you install the app, follow the guides in Documentation: Install on Desktop or Install on Mobile, then First Login.
