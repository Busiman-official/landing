Open the blog with **Blog** in the footer. The page heading is **Blog**. (The browser tab reads "Blog: Practical Guides for Running a Small Business".) Each row shows the title, a short summary, the date and a reading time such as "5 min read". Reading times are estimates based on length.

There are three articles. They are not in a fixed order:

- Accounting vs. Cash Flow: Why Every Business Owner Should Know the Difference
- How to Manage a Small Business Effectively: A Complete Guide for Indian Business Owners (2026)
- How to Manage Inventory Effectively: A Complete Guide for Small Businesses

Inside an article you see:

- **← All posts** to go back to the list
- The date and reading time
- **On this page**, a list of headings. On the blog it comes before the article text.
- A reading progress bar

The articles have no pictures and no author name.
