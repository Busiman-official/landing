## Privacy Policy

Open it with **Privacy Policy** in the footer. It was last updated on 7 October 2026. It has a list of sections. On a wide screen the list is a column on the left. On smaller screens it moves above the text. The sections are:

- Who we are
- Our role
- Information we collect
- How we use information
- Who we share information with
- Where your information is stored
- How long we keep information
- How we protect information
- Your rights
- Children
- Changes to this policy
- Contact us

Key points from the policy:

- The website "does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers". The web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.
- Busiman does not sell your information, does not show ads and does not send marketing emails.
- Your business controls its own business information. Many requests are fastest through your business admin. You can also write to team@busiman.org.

## Terms of Service

Open it with **Terms** in the footer. It was last updated on 14 October 2026. It links to the Privacy Policy and to the Delete Your Account page.

## Deleting Your Account

The Delete Your Account page is **not** in the header or the footer. Open it directly:

**busiman.org/delete-account**

You can also reach it from the **Terms** page. In the section "Ending your use", tap the link "Delete Your Account".

The page says the following (last updated 8 October 2026):

- If you are an **employee**, your business admin controls your account. The admin can remove it right away from **Employees** in the app.
- If you are a **business admin** who wants the whole company account deleted, or you cannot reach your admin, email **team@busiman.org**.

**How to request deletion**

1. Send an email to team@busiman.org. Use the email address linked to your account. Or send the same request from the phone number registered on the account.
2. Include your registered phone number or email address.
3. Include your company name, as it shows in the app.
4. Busiman will confirm your request and complete it "promptly".

There is no deletion form on the website and no deadline is given other than "promptly".

**What gets deleted**

- Your account, login details and session history
- Your attendance, call, sales, purchase, inventory and service report records
- Any fingerprint data enrolled against your account
- Photos, signatures and files you uploaded through the app

**What may be kept**

- Copies of deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for some time before they are permanently purged.
- Records the law requires Busiman to keep (for example, under tax or labour law) are kept only as long as the law requires, and only for that purpose.
