The Download section is on the home page. Its small label says "Get started". Its heading is "Your office, organised by this evening." Below it: "Download Busiman on the machine at the desk or the phone in your pocket. It is free, and it stays free."

You see two cards: Desktop and Mobile. On a computer they sit side by side. On a phone, Mobile is below Desktop.

| Card | Tagline under the title | What it says | Buttons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Desktop** | Windows & macOS | "The full app for the office computer at the front desk." | **Windows**, **macOS** |
| **Mobile** | Android & iOS | "Attendance, calls and service reports from the field." | **Android**, and a dimmed **iOS** label with a dashed outline, marked **Soon** |

Things to know:

- Each real button opens in a new browser tab.
- The website does not guess your device. Both cards always show. Pick the one that matches the device you want to install on.
- If a version number is available, it shows under the buttons, for example "v1.2.3".
- **iOS** is not a button. It is marked **Soon** because the iPhone app is coming soon. You cannot download it yet.

## Install on Windows

1. In the **Desktop** card, click **Windows**. A file ending in `.exe` downloads.
2. Open the downloaded file and follow the prompts. You do not need administrator rights for a per-user install.
3. Windows may show a warning about an "unknown publisher". This is expected. The Windows version is not yet code-signed, which is a kind of digital stamp. The warning does not mean the file is broken.
4. To continue, click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.
5. Finish the installation, then open Busiman.

> **Note:** The wording of the warning comes from Windows, so it may look slightly different on your computer.

## Install on macOS

1. In the **Desktop** card, click **macOS**. A file ending in `.dmg` downloads.
2. Open the file.
3. Drag Busiman into your **Applications** folder.

## Install on Android

1. On your Android phone, scroll to the **Mobile** card and tap **Android**.
2. Follow the steps on your phone to finish the installation.
3. When you first use some features, the app asks for permission. It asks only when needed, not all at once:
   - **Location**: only when marking attendance, to record where a check-in happened
   - **Camera**: for scanning inventory items and taking service report photos
   - **Bluetooth**: for automatic check-in near a gate device, if your office has one
   - **Notifications**: for alerts, such as an attendance approval request

None of these are required to use the app in general. The app asks again at the moment a feature needs one.

> **If no download starts:** Contact the team at team@busiman.org or +91 75006 73358 and ask for the installer.

## Install on iPhone or iPad

The iOS app is not available yet. The home page shows **Soon**. The FAQ says: "The iOS app is not available yet."

## Updating the desktop app

The desktop app checks for updates when it starts. If an update is ready, a box appears with **Download Now** or **Later**. The update installs the next time you close the app. You never need to reinstall by hand. You can see your current version in **Settings → Account** inside the app.
