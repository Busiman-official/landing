This page explains what the Busiman website (busiman.org) does and does not do with your information, and how to stay safe when you use it. It is written for visitors and for customers who open a shared service report link. For the full legal terms, read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## The short version

- The website has no forms, no login and no sign-up. It does not ask you to type anything in.
- The website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
- The website code does not set cookies.
- A shared service report link is private, but anyone who holds the link can open the report. Treat it like a key.
- Get the Busiman apps only from the download buttons on busiman.org.
- If you are unsure about anything, write to team@busiman.org or call +91 75006 73358.

## What the website collects from you

**Nothing through forms.** There is no contact form. To reach us, you tap the phone number or email address, and your own phone or email app takes over. Anything you send us that way is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

**No analytics, ads or cookies.** The site has no analytics tools and no advertising trackers. The website code sets no cookies. This matches our Privacy Policy, which says the website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

**One temporary note in your browser tab.** When you open the homepage, a short welcome screen appears. So that it does not repeat every time, the site saves a tiny note in your browser tab that says the welcome screen has already been shown. The note holds no personal information. It stays only in your browser, it applies to that one tab, and it goes away when you close the tab.

**Standard server logs.** Like most websites, the company that hosts the site may keep ordinary logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested. They are used to keep the site running and secure. This is also described in the Privacy Policy. We describe them here in the same words as our Privacy Policy.

## What the website fetches from other services

The download buttons on the homepage show the newest version of each app. To do this, the website asks two services for the latest release details:

- **GitHub**, for the Windows and macOS installers.
- **Expo**, for the Android app and its version.

These requests are made by the website's own server, not by your browser. Nothing about you or your device is included in these requests. GitHub and Expo see only the website's own server asking for the latest release details. The results are kept for a short time, about an hour for desktop and about half an hour for Android, so the buttons stay current.

If one of these services is not available, the buttons still take you somewhere useful. They lead to the general GitHub releases page or the Expo project page instead. You will not see an error message.

Your browser contacts GitHub or Expo directly only when you click a download button. Those buttons open in a new tab. From that point, the other service's own privacy rules apply to your visit.

## Downloading the apps safely

**Use only the buttons on busiman.org.** Open [busiman.org](https://www.busiman.org), go to the Download section, and use the buttons there. Do not install Busiman from links in messages, emails or other websites, even if they look official.

**Windows may show a warning.** The first time you run the Windows installer, Windows SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher". This happens because the Busiman installer is not yet code-signed. Code-signing is a paid certificate that tells Windows who made a program. The warning does not by itself mean the file is harmful, but it is a good reason to be sure you downloaded from the Busiman website. If you did, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**If the Android button opens an Expo page instead of starting a download,** contact us at team@busiman.org or +91 75006 73358 and we will help you get the installer.

**iOS is coming soon.** There is no iPhone app yet. The iOS chip on the page is not a download link.

**If you are unsure,** stop and ask. Email team@busiman.org or call +91 75006 73358 before you install.

## Shared service reports

A business that uses Busiman can share a service report with its customer as a link. Here is how that works.

**The link is private, not locked.** The report is not listed anywhere on the website or in search results, so people normally reach it only through the link. There is no password and no login. This means anyone who holds the link can open the report. Our Privacy Policy says the same: share these links only with the intended customer.

**What the report page shows.** Depending on the report, the page can show:

- the business name and logo, and the branch name, address and phone number
- the report number and the completion date
- the customer's name
- the technician's name
- machine names, serial numbers and the problem described
- parts used and parts removed, with quantities
- the payment summary: parts, service charge, amount received and any amount pending

**Handled by Busiman's servers.** The website does not keep the report. Each time someone opens the link, the website asks Busiman's servers for the report and shows it. Checking that a link is valid is handled by Busiman's servers. How long a link keeps working is not something this website controls.

You may see one of two pages if the report does not open:

- **"This page could not be found."** The link may have stopped working. Ask the business that sent it for a new link.
- **"This page couldn't load" with a Reload button.** The problem is on our side. Try again a little later. If it keeps happening, contact us. You do not need a new link.

**Please do not share the link publicly.** Do not post it on social media, in public groups or on open web pages. Send it only to the person it is meant for.

**Link previews in chat apps.** When you paste a report link into a chat app, the app may show a small preview. That preview can include the report number, the business name and logo, the customer's name and the machine names. Anyone in that chat can see it. Before you paste a report link into a group, think about who is in it.

**Search engines.** The report page asks search engines not to list it, and our robots file asks them to stay away from report links. Search engines are not forced to obey those requests, so the best protection is still to keep the link to yourself.

**Download PDF.** The "Download PDF" button at the bottom of the report opens your browser's print window. Choose "Save as PDF" there. On some phones the print window may not offer "Save as PDF". If so, open the link on a computer. Nothing is uploaded, and the website does not create the file. The button itself does not appear on the PDF. Your browser may add a header and footer to the page, and these can include the report's web address, which is the private link. To avoid this, turn off **Headers and footers** in the print window before you save.

**Your browser remembers the link.** Like any web address, the link can appear in your browser history. If you open a report on a shared or public computer, close the tab and clear your history afterwards.

## Deleting your account

You can ask for your Busiman account to be deleted at any time. The steps are on the [Delete Your Account](/delete-account) page.

If you are an employee of a business that uses Busiman, the fastest way is to ask your business admin. They can remove you right away from **Employees** in the app. The steps below are for a business admin who wants the whole company account deleted, or for anyone who cannot reach their admin.

1. Email team@busiman.org from the email address linked to your account, or send the request from the phone number registered on the account.
2. Include your registered phone number or email address, and your company name as it appears in the app.
3. We will confirm the request and complete it promptly.

The deletion page explains what is deleted and what may be kept for a while, such as copies in backups and records the law requires us to keep. There is no deletion form on the website.
