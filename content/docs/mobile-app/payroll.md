Payroll is an owner tool. Open **Payroll** from Quick Links. Admins always see it. The tile shows for others only if the Payroll department is on for them, and the employee form does not normally offer it.

## Run a month

1. On **Payroll**, find the card **Start a month**. It builds a payslip for every salaried employee from attendance, overtime and advances.
2. Tap **Month** and choose the month (the last 12 months are listed). Tap **Create run**.
3. Open the run from the **RUNS** list. Tap **Calculate payroll** (or **Recalculate**).
4. Check the totals: **Payslips**, **Gross** and **Net pay**. Open a payslip to see **Earnings**, **Deductions**, **Paid leave** and **Adjustments**. You can type **Paid leave days** and **Save**, and add allowances or deductions with the round **+**.
5. Tap **Submit for approval**.
6. An admin taps **Approve run**, or **Send back for changes** (with a reason).
7. After approval, tap **Record payout** on each payslip: Cash, UPI or Bank transfer.
8. An admin taps **Lock run** to freeze it. Locking applies advance recoveries. Payouts can still be recorded after locking.

Run statuses: **Not calculated**, **Ready to submit**, **Waiting for approval**, **Approved** and **Locked**. Payslips show **Unpaid**, **Part paid** or **Paid**.

> **Who can do what:** Only admins see **Approve run**, **Send back for changes** and **Lock run**. Anyone who can open Payroll sees the other buttons. Non-admins see the line "Waiting for an admin to approve this run."

## Setting a salary

Salary is set when creating an employee, in the **Salary (optional)** card: salary type, amount, **Overtime multiplier** and **Effective from**. **Edit employee** has no salary fields, so you cannot change it later from the app.

## Payslips for employees

Busiman does not have an employee screen to view your own payslip, and a payslip cannot be shared as a PDF. See "Not available yet".
