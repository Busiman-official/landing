---
title: "Alerts on Mobile"
description: "The bell icon shows your alerts, and tapping one takes you to the right place in the app."
status: "live"
platform: "mobile"
---

Tap the bell for **Alerts**. The heading shows a count such as "2 alerts". When empty, you see "No pending alerts" ("You're all caught up.") or "No alerts for your account".

Tapping an alert takes you to the right place: an attendance approval opens the approval sheet; a job alert opens the job; a sale opens Sales; stock expiry opens Inventory; a received purchase opens Purchases; an advance opens **My advances**; a business number alert opens **Virtual numbers**.

Two things to know:

- People who can approve attendance (admin, HR, manager, attendance officer) see only attendance approvals, tasks, sales, stock expiry and received purchases in the list. Other alerts, such as service jobs, outsourcing, advances and business numbers, still reach their phone as notifications.
- Everyone else sees all alerts except attendance approval requests.
