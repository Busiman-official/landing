## Sign-in and account

| Message or problem | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number. | The number must be 10 digits. |
| This account isn't active — contact your admin. | Your account is switched off. Ask your admin. |
| Enter your password. | The password box is empty. |
| Choose which company to log into. | Pick a company from the **Company** list. |
| Incorrect password. | Check the password, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| Too many attempts. Please wait a minute and try again. | Wait a minute and try again. |
| You are offline. Connect to the internet to sign in. | Reconnect to Wi-Fi or mobile data. |
| You appear to be offline. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then try again. | Same as above. |
| No connection to the server. Check internet access, VPN, or try again when you have better signal. | Check your internet or VPN. |
| This is taking too long. Check your connection and try again. | Your connection is slow. Try again. |
| Enter the 4-digit code. / Incorrect code. | Type all 4 digits, or tap **Resend code**. |
| Could not resend the code. Please try again. | Wait and tap **Resend code** again. |
| Password must be at least 8 characters. / Passwords do not match. | Fix the new password. |
| Could not update your password. Start over. | Begin **Forgot password?** again. |
| An error message appears when I log in and I am an employee. | If your admin logged you out, you are locked. Ask your admin to tap **Allow login again**. |
| I am suddenly on the login screen. | Your session ended. Sign in again. If you cannot, ask your admin to allow login again. |

## Attendance

| Message or problem | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| Scanning for office gate… | The app is looking for the gate. Stay near the door. |
| Bluetooth permission needed... / Turn on Bluetooth... | Tap the message and allow. |
| Can't check in remotely | You cannot send a remote request. Move closer to the gate or ask your manager to enable it. |
| Select which job this is for. | Choose a job, or tap **Not this? Enter an explanation instead**. |
| Please enter at least one letter in your explanation. | The reason cannot be empty. |
| Please specify where you are marking from. | You chose **Other**. Type the place. |
| Pending Approval | Wait for your manager, or tap **Cancel Request**. |
| Request rejected | Read the reason. Try again if you can. |
| You don't have a shift assigned for today... | Ask your manager or HR to assign a shift. |
| Reason required / at least 10 characters (approvers) | Write a longer rejection reason. |

## Everyday use

| Message or problem | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| Something went wrong / Unable to load team data | Tap **Try Again**. |
| Couldn't load jobs / Couldn't load stock. Pull down to try again. | Tap **Retry** or pull down to refresh. |
| Offline — your session is kept. Reconnect to sync. | You have no connection. Reconnect and repeat the task. |
| Network error ... or "Unable to connect to the server..." | Check your internet and try again. |
| You don't have permission to perform this action. | Ask your manager or admin. |
| Customer required / Select a customer before creating the job. | Choose a customer first. |
| Signature required | Ask the customer to sign before you submit. |
| Pending + received must equal ₹... | Fix the amounts so they add up to the total. |
| No sales counter available | Ask your admin to set up or assign a counter. |
| No counter has an open session right now | Open the register first. |
| Calling isn't turned on for you | Ask HR or your admin for a calling extension. |
| Phone line paused | Tap **Fix & connect**. |
| Camera permission is required to scan barcodes | Tap **Grant Permission**. |
| Turn on notifications | Tap **Open Settings** and allow notifications. |
| The screen flickered and I am back on Home | You do not have access to that screen. Ask your admin. |

Some messages are written by the Busiman servers. They can change, so this guide does not quote them. If a message appears and you do not understand it, take a screenshot and show your admin.

## Still stuck

The footer on Home says "Need a hand? We're here." Tap it to email or phone the Busiman team.
