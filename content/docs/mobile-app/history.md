The **History** tab is a log of business activity.

- Search with "Search history…".
- The filter button (an icon beside the search box, with a blue dot when active) opens date choices **Today**, **Last 7 days** (the default) and **Last 30 days**, or a custom range, and modules (**Attendance**, **Sales**, **Purchases**, **Inventory**, **Expenses** and **Service**). Attendance is always listed; the others follow the departments your branch uses. Tap **Apply**, or **Reset**.
- Entries are grouped under **Today**, **Yesterday** and dates. When new activity arrives, a pill says "New activity — tap to refresh".
- Tap an Attendance entry to open that person's attendance page (people without team access are sent to Home). Tap any other entry to see its details in a popup with **OK**.
- Empty: "No activity in this range".

Which entries you see depends on your role and is decided by your company's Busiman server.
