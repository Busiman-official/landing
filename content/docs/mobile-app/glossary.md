---
title: "Mobile Glossary"
description: "Plain-language meanings of the terms used in the Busiman mobile app."
status: "live"
platform: "mobile"
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | The owner-level role. Sees everything and manages people and company settings. |
| **Advance** | Money the company gives an employee that is later taken from salary or paid back. |
| **Alerts** | The list behind the bell icon. |
| **Attendance officer** | Someone an admin lets approve and mark attendance. |
| **Bills waiting** | Service bills sent to the counter for the cashier to finish. |
| **B2B** | Business-to-business: selling to another company instead of a walk-in customer. |
| **BOM** | Bill of materials: the standard list of parts a machine uses. |
| **Branch** | One location or unit of your company. |
| **Counter** | A billing point with a cash drawer. |
| **Department** | A feature area switched on for a branch, such as Sales or Payroll. |
| **Enquiry** | A possible sale that you track before it becomes a quotation or sale. |
| **Extension** | Your calling number inside the company phone system. |
| **Gate** | A device at the office door that proves you are there using Bluetooth. |
| **GST rate** | The tax percentage charged on a product. |
| **Hold order** | Parking a cart on your phone to finish later. |
| **HSN code** | A standard number that classifies a product for tax. |
| **Kit** | The parts and serial items a technician carries. |
| **Machine** | An item on a service job that needs servicing. |
| **Outsourcing** | Sending parts or machines to an outside vendor to work on. |
| **Pending Approval** | A remote request waiting for a manager's decision. |
| **Quick Links** | The grid of tiles on Home. |
| **RMA number** | A reference number for a returned item, given by the supplier. |
| **Register** | The cash session for a counter. You open it and close it. |
| **Remote check-in** | A check-in request you send with a reason, approved by a manager. |
| **Role** | Admin, manager, HR or employee. |
| **Serial number** | A unique number on one unit of a product. |
| **Service center** | A place that handles repair jobs. |
| **Service job (report)** | A record of a repair or visit, from request to payment. |
| **SKU** | A short code for a product or variant. |
| **Udhar** | Money a customer owes you, or you owe, on account. |
| **Variant** | A version of a product, such as size or colour. |
| **Virtual number** | A business phone number for your company. |
| **Walk-in** | A sale with no saved customer. |
