The bar has five controls: **Home**, **Call**, **Scan**, **Kit** and **History**.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your dashboard |
| **Call** | The phone dialer (see "Calling") |
| **Scan** (the round button in the middle with a QR symbol and no words) | Opens the barcode scanner. **Employees see a message instead**, see "Not available yet" |
| **Kit** | **My Kit**, the parts you carry (see "Service jobs") |
| **History** | The business activity log (see "History") |

A small red dot on **History** means there is new activity since you last opened it.

Everything else (Sales, Service, Expenses, Purchases, Parties, Inventory, Payroll) is reached from the Quick Links on Home. Alerts is the bell, and Profile is your avatar.

> **Kit note:** If your branch does not use the Service department, tapping **Kit** shows a quick spinner and then brings you back to Home. No message appears.

## The scanner

Admins, managers and HR can use **Scan**. Employees cannot (see "Not available yet").

1. The first time, Busiman says "Camera permission is required to scan barcodes". Tap **Grant Permission**, then allow the camera.
2. Point the camera at a barcode inside the frame. Or tap **Manual Entry** and type the code into "Enter barcode or code". **Continuous Scan Mode** keeps scanning one code after another.
3. A sheet shows what Busiman found: an item, a serial number, a location, or "Unknown Barcode" ("This barcode is not registered in inventory.").

Some scanner actions work and some lead nowhere. These work:

- **View** and **View Stock by Location** on an item or serial.
- **Count** and **Count Location**, which start a stock count.
- **Create New Product** on an unknown barcode.
- Scanning from inside a sale, a service job or a purchase. Those add the item or code to what you are doing.

Other actions (for example **Transfer**, **Damage**, **Waste**, **Receive Here**, **Transfer From Here**, **Register Serial**) lead to a screen that does not exist yet. See "Not available yet".
