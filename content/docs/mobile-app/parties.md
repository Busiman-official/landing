**Parties** holds your customers, suppliers and outsourcing partners. Open it from Quick Links. It shows if your branch has Sales, Service jobs or Purchases. The tabs you see depend on your department and role:

| Tab | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Customers** | Sales or Service department |
| **Suppliers** | Purchases department |
| **Outsourcing** | Admin or manager, with the Service department |

Tap a row to open it. From a customer you can call, send a WhatsApp message or email. You can also edit details and add team notes.
