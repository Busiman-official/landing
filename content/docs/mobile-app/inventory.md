Open **Inventory** from Quick Links. You need the Inventory department.

- **Stock list**: search with "Search products…". Tap the location filter, **Show stock at**, to see stock at one place. If loading fails, a message says "Couldn't load stock. Pull down to try again."
- **Add a product**: tap the round **+** on the stock list, or use the blue **+** > **Add new product**. On the **Product** section, add **Product name**, **Unit**, **Category**, **Type** and **Serviceable**. In **Variants**, add a **SKU** (suggested for you), a **Label**, a **Variant type**, **Serial tracking** (**Not tracked**, **Required (every unit)** or **Optional (assign at sale/dispatch)**), a **HSN code** and a **GST rate %**. Tap **Save product**. This form has no price and no opening stock. Stock arrives through purchases.
- **Add a variant** to an existing product from its item page: give a **Variant name**, optionally a **SKU** and **Barcode (optional)**.
- **Product menu**: on the stock list, open a product's menu for **Count this product**, **Edit product**, **Add variant** and **Delete product** (confirm with **Delete permanently**). The round count button on the stock list starts a stock count.
- **Item page**: shows stock by location and variants. It also has **Add variant**, **Count**, **Scan** and a pencil button to edit.
- **Locations** (admin): Profile > Company > **Locations** lists warehouses, racks and other places stock lives. Admins can add and edit them.

## Counting stock

The screen is titled **Stock count**. Reach it from Inventory or from the scanner's **Count** actions.

1. **Where are you counting?** Choose a location and tap **Start count at ...**.
2. **Counting**: type the **Qty** for each product. A dash means you skipped it.
3. **Review before it's final**: check the totals and tap **Edit counts** if needed.
4. Tap **Submit count**.
