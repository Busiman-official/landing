Open **Expenses** from Quick Links. You need the Expenses department.

## Adding an expense

Tap the round **+** on the list, or use the blue **+** > **New expense**. The screen is titled **Add Expense**.

1. Type the **amount** in ₹. It must be more than 0.
2. In **Paid to**, type the vendor, or start typing a colleague's name.
3. Choose a **Category**. The first chips are Chai, Petrol, Parcel and VTC. After those come categories like Rent, Utilities and Miscellaneous. If you choose **Miscellaneous**, describe the expense.
4. How it is paid:
   - **Admins** pick **Paid via**: **Cash**, **UPI** or **Bank**. Cash needs an open counter.
   - **Everyone else** sees a **Counter** card. The expense is deducted from that counter's cash. If no counter is open, Busiman says to open a counter session first.
5. Add an optional **Note**.
6. Tap **Save**.

There is no way to attach a photo or receipt.

## Advances

If you pick a colleague's name in **Paid to**, the expense becomes an **advance**. Busiman warns you: "This will be logged as an advance (udhar) to ...". You can set **Recover from salary each month (₹)**. If you leave it empty, payroll takes the full amount in one go.

If you have an advance, you see a red "You owe ₹..." card on Home and **My advances** in your Profile. **My advances** shows "₹... outstanding" or "All settled up".

Admins and managers also see an **Employee advances** card on the Expenses list. It leads to a list by person with statuses **Outstanding**, **Partially repaid** and **Repaid**. Use **Collect repayment** to record money coming back.

## Approving expenses

Rows show a **Pending** or **Rejected** badge. Approved rows show no badge. Open a row to see **Expense details**. Admins and managers can tap **Approve** or **Reject** (with an optional reason). The person who added it, or an approver, can delete it.
