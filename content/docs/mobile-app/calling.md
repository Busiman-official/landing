Busiman can make and receive business calls over the internet, using a calling extension from your company.

## Getting a line

Your admin gives you a calling extension. You cannot switch it on yourself. If you have none, the **Call** tab says "Calling isn't turned on for you". It says to ask HR or your admin to enable app calling and assign an extension. Everything else in Busiman still works.

Admins can switch it on for themselves: **Profile** > **My calling extension** > **Turn on** > a message says "Calling is on".

## Making a call

1. Tap **Call**. You may see "Loading phone…" and "Connecting phone service…".
2. Type a number on the keypad. The display reads "Enter number" when empty. Tap the delete button to remove the last digit.
3. Tap the round green phone button in the middle of the dial pad. While the call is live, that button turns red; tap it to hang up. **Speaker** sits to its left.
4. Tap **Contacts** to pick someone. You can search "Search team or personal contacts". The first time, allow Busiman to read your phone's contacts.

At the top of the dial pad, above the keys, a line shows the state of your line: "Registered", "Connecting…", "Waiting for internet…", "Could not sign in" or "Reconnecting…". The dial pad also shows a row of team members you can call. When the line has problems, help buttons appear: **Refresh phone login** or **Try again now**.

The full-screen call view also has **Keypad**, **Speaker**, **Add call**, **Bluetooth** and **More**.

## Phone line paused

If you see **Phone line paused**, Busiman could not sign in to your line. It tries again by itself. You can also tap **Fix & connect** to refresh your login right away. If it keeps happening, close Busiman on any other phone that uses the same extension, then tap **Fix & connect**. Note that **Fix & connect** can reset the line's password, so other phones using the same extension will need their own fix.

## Incoming calls

You need a working line, notifications allowed and, on newer Android, full-screen alerts allowed. Busiman may ask: "Allow full-screen calls". Tap **Open Settings** and allow it. Without it, incoming calls show as a normal notification.

## Call history

Tap the history button in **Contacts** to open **Call history**. It has two tabs:

- **This phone**: calls made or received on this phone. It is kept on the phone, up to 200 entries.
- **Business calls**: calls from the company's record.

## Virtual numbers

A **virtual number** is a business phone number for your company. Open **Profile** > **Virtual numbers**. This row shows only when your company has virtual numbers switched on, for admins too. The tabs are **My numbers** and **Requests**. Tap **Ask for a number** (a preferred city is optional) and **Send request**. You can rename a number with **Edit number**. If there is none yet: "No numbers yet". Notifications tell you when a number is assigned or turned down.
