Open **Purchases** from Quick Links. You need the Purchases department. The tabs are **Dashboard**, **Orders** and **Returns**.

## Creating a purchase order

1. Tap the round button to start a new purchase order.
2. Search with "Search products to add…". The date chip, **Today**, is the entry date.
3. In **Order details**, choose a **Select supplier** (you can add a new one on the spot), an **Expected delivery** date, a **Priority** (**Low**, **Normal** or **Urgent**) and **Notes (optional)**.
4. Add products to **Your order**. Set quantities. **Expected price (optional)** is a note, not a tax or cost field.
5. Tap **Create order**. The order is confirmed and Busiman opens the receiving screens.

## Receiving goods

The **Receive goods** screen opens when you start a new order, or when you open an order from the list.

1. On **Items**, set the quantity received for each line. Add serial numbers where required. Add a **Unit price (optional)**. You can add extra items.
2. Tap **Next: Review & pay**.
3. Optionally add a **Supplier invoice number** and **Delivery note number**. Add **Shipping / freight** and **Notes**. Enter the payment for each method.
4. Tap **Complete**.

**Quick receive.** To receive goods with no purchase order, use the **Quick receive** card on the Purchases Dashboard ("Receive goods from a supplier — no order needed"). It opens the same **Receive goods** screen.

A summary shows what happened:

- **Order completed**: everything arrived.
- **Partially received**: some items are still open. Tap **Receive remaining** later.
- **Receipt posted**: a receipt with no purchase order.

The summary also has **Create return** and **Back to orders**. To cancel an order, tap the red **Cancel order** and confirm.

## Purchase returns

1. Tap **Create return** (or use the **Returns** tab).
2. On **Return items**, pick the lines and tap **Continue →**.
3. On **Return details**, choose where the goods leave from, pick a reason, add an optional RMA number and notes.
4. Tap **Save as draft** or **Submit return**.

Suppliers are managed under **Parties**.
