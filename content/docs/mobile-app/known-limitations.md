---
title: "Known Limitations on Mobile"
description: "Things the Busiman mobile app cannot do or does differently from what you might expect, with workarounds where they exist."
status: "live"
platform: "mobile"
---

- **Offline use**: you need an internet connection to sign in and to save anything.
- **No biometric login or app lock.**
- **Passwords**: 8 characters when signing up or resetting, 6 when changing it or when an admin creates an employee.
- **Update popup** cannot be dismissed.
- **Non-admins cannot log out.**
- **Branch switching** is for admins who have two or more branches.
- **Managers**: the **Material edit requests** card sends you to Home when you tap its body.
- **Attendance calendar**: no month or day-by-day view exists.
- **Create button**: the blue **+** only shows with the Service department, even though it offers other items.
- **Expense approval switch** changes nothing visible.
- **Payroll** has no employee payslip screen.
- **Alerts** list hides some types from approvers.
- **Calendar** department can be chosen at signup, but there is no Calendar screen.
- **Voice input**: voice buttons depend on your phone's speech support. If it fails, type instead.
- **Some wording** (for example server messages) can appear in plain English or mixed language. If a message is unclear, tell your admin.
