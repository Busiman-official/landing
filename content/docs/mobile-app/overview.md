> Run your business from your phone. Check in to work, make sales, manage service jobs, track stock, record expenses, run payroll and make business calls, all in one app.

## Overview

Busiman is a business app for companies and their teams. Owners, managers, HR staff, office staff and field engineers all use the same app. What you see depends on your **role** and on which **departments** your branch uses.

This guide explains how Busiman works from your first sign-in to everyday tasks. It also lists what the app cannot do yet, so nothing surprises you.

> **Good to know:** Screens, buttons and messages in this guide use the same words you see in the app. Some buttons are only icons with no words. This guide describes those by what they look like.
