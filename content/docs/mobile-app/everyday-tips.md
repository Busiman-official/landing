- Open Busiman on Home when you arrive at work so the gate can find you.
- Keep Bluetooth on if your workplace uses a gate.
- Allow notifications. You will otherwise miss approvals and shift reminders.
- Pull down on a list to refresh it.
- If a button is only an icon, look at its colour and position. The guide names each one.
- Typed-in forms are not saved if you close the app. A failed save keeps the form open with a message, so you can try again.
- You cannot log yourself out unless you are an admin. If you need to be signed out of a phone, ask your admin.

## If more than one person uses the same phone

Busiman does not clear everything when someone is signed out. These stay on the phone for the next person:

- **Call history**, up to 200 calls, in **Call history** > **This phone**.
- Held sales carts on a counter ("Hold order") and recently used items.
- Where you placed the floating check-in pill.
- Exported attendance files and shared reports.

Advice for shared phones:

1. Prefer one phone per person.
2. If you must share, ask your admin to end the previous person's session first.
3. Do not keep held sales carts with customer details you want private.
4. Delete exported files you no longer need.
5. Use your phone's own screen lock.

## The offline strip

When your phone has no connection, a yellow strip at the top says "Offline — your session is kept. Reconnect to sync." Busiman does not queue your work. Nothing is sent later on its own. Reconnect, then do the task again. The only exception is a held sales cart, which stays on your phone.
