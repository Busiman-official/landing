This policy explains what information Busiman collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers:

- the Busiman mobile app for Android and iOS
- the Busiman desktop app for Windows and macOS
- the Busiman attendance gate and fingerprint sensor
- this website, [busiman.org](https://www.busiman.org), including shared service report links

It does not cover websites or apps that other businesses build or run, even if they use Busiman behind the scenes.

> **Note:** Busiman is used by businesses to manage their own staff, customers and stock. If you are an employee or a customer of a business that uses Busiman, that business decides what goes into its account. For questions about that data, please contact the business first. We will help them answer you.

## Who we are

Busiman is a free office management app built and run in India. In this policy, "Busiman", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the Busiman service. "Your business" means the company or organisation whose Busiman account you use.

You can reach us at [team@busiman.org](mailto:team@busiman.org) or **+91 75006 73358**.

## Our role

We handle two kinds of information, and our role is different for each.

- **Account information** (your login, phone number, sessions and devices). We decide how this is used, so we are responsible for it.
- **Business information** (employees, attendance, customers, sales, stock, service reports and so on). Your business decides what to store and who can see it. We store and process it only to run Busiman for that business.

This matches the roles set out in India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: your business is the "data fiduciary" for its business information, and we process it on its behalf.

## Information we collect

### Account and login

- Your **phone number** and **password**. We confirm new phone numbers with a one-time code sent by SMS. Passwords and codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
- An **email address** for business admins, which we confirm with a one-time code.
- **Session details**: the device and browser you signed in from, your IP address, and when you signed in. These keep your account secure and let you sign out of other devices.

### Employee records

When your business adds you as an employee, it may store:

- name, phone, email, address, date of birth, profile photo and a short bio
- employee ID, job title, joining date, branch, manager and shift
- an emergency contact
- payroll details, payslips and salary advances

### Attendance

Busiman offers several ways to mark attendance. Depending on what your business turns on, we may record:

- **check-in and check-out times**
- **location** (GPS coordinates and the matching street address) at the moment you mark attendance from your phone
- the **Wi-Fi network** your device is connected to, used to confirm you are at the office
- a **device ID** made from your phone or computer's details, so attendance can only be marked from devices your business approves
- notes you type or speak for remote attendance

We **do not track your location in the background**. Location is read only when you mark attendance. While you are signed in, the mobile app uses **Bluetooth** to detect a nearby Busiman attendance gate. It does not track where you go.

The **desktop app** can mark attendance automatically when your office computer starts, wakes up, shuts down or connects to the office network, if your business turns this on.

### Fingerprints

If your business uses the Busiman fingerprint gate:

- Your fingerprint is scanned and stored **only on the sensor inside the gate**. It is never sent to our servers or the apps.
- Our servers store only which employee is linked to which sensor slot, and a record of your **consent** (when you agreed, and who enrolled you).
- When an employee is removed, their fingerprints are deleted from the gate first.

Your fingerprint is not enrolled without your consent. You can ask your business to remove it at any time.

### Business information

Your business can store information about its own customers, suppliers and operations, such as:

- customer and supplier names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and GST numbers
- quotations, invoices, sales, purchases, expenses and payment status
- service reports, machine details, photos and customer signatures
- the company's bank account and UPI details, used on invoices
- stock and product images

### Calls

Busiman includes an in-app phone system for business calls.

- To show names in the dialer, the app reads the **contacts on your phone**. Your contacts stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
- Your **call history** in the app is stored on your phone.
- Calls go through Busiman's own phone system, which may keep basic call records such as the numbers involved, time and duration.

### Phone and computer permissions

The apps ask for permission before using:

| Permission | Why it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Marking attendance at the right place |
| Bluetooth | Detecting the attendance gate |
| Microphone | Business calls and speaking attendance notes |
| Speech recognition | Turning spoken notes into text |
| Contacts | Showing names in the dialer |
| Phone | Placing and receiving business calls |
| Camera and photos | Scanning barcodes, profile photos, company logo and service report photos |
| Notifications | Shift reminders, approvals and incoming calls |

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. Some features will stop working without them.

### This website

This website **does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers**. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure.

## How we use information

We use information only to:

- create and secure your account and confirm your phone number and email
- run the features your business uses: attendance, payroll, calls, sales, stock, service reports and so on
- send notifications you need, such as shift reminders, approvals and incoming calls
- deliver app updates
- prevent misuse, fraud and unauthorised access
- fix problems and keep the service running
- meet legal obligations

We **do not sell** your information. We **do not show ads** and we do not use your information for advertising. We do not send marketing emails.

## Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Busiman, and only what each one needs:

| Provider | What it does for us |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | Sending verification emails and storing quotation PDFs |
| Fly.io | Hosting the Busiman servers |
| MongoDB | Database for business and account information |
| Cloudinary | Storing logos, product images and documents |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivering notifications to your phone |
| MessageCentral | Sending one-time codes by SMS |
| Expo | Delivering mobile app updates |
| GitHub | Delivering desktop app updates |
| Apple and Google | Speech-to-text on your phone, when you dictate a note |

We may also share information:

- **within your business**, with people your business admin has given access to
- **when the law requires it**, such as a valid order from a court or government authority
- **if Busiman is merged or sold**, in which case this policy will continue to protect your information

### Shared service report links

Your business can share a service report with a customer as a link. **Anyone who has the link can open the report.** The report may show the customer's name, machine details, amounts and the technician's name. Share these links only with the intended customer.

## Where your information is stored

Busiman's information is stored with the providers listed above. Some of it is stored in India (for example, emails and documents on AWS in Mumbai). Some providers may store or process information in other countries. When information leaves India, we rely on providers with strong security practices and only send what is needed.

On your devices:

- the mobile app keeps login details in your phone's secure storage
- the desktop app keeps login details in your computer's encrypted storage
- the desktop app keeps a short activity log for automatic attendance, which it deletes after 7 days

## How long we keep information

- **Business information** is kept while your business uses Busiman, or until your business deletes it.
- When an admin **removes an employee**, that person's account, sessions, activity log, fingerprints and phone extension are deleted.
- When a business **deletes its company account**, all of its information is deleted from our live systems. Copies may remain in backups for some time.
- **One-time codes** delete themselves shortly after they expire. **Sign-in sessions** delete themselves when they expire.

## How we protect information

- Passwords and one-time codes are stored only in hashed form.
- Phone system passwords are encrypted.
- Each business's information is kept in its own separate database.
- Login details on phones and computers are kept in the device's secure storage.
- Attendance gates prove their identity to our servers with signed messages.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- **know** what personal information we hold about you and how it is used
- **correct or update** information that is wrong or incomplete
- **erase** information that is no longer needed
- **withdraw consent**, for example for fingerprint attendance
- **have a grievance addressed**
- **nominate** someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act

Because your business controls its business information, many requests are fastest through your business admin. You can also write to us at [team@busiman.org](mailto:team@busiman.org). We will work with your business to respond.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

Busiman is a workplace tool and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child's information has been added, contact us and we will help remove it.
