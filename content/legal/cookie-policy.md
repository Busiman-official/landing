This policy explains how cookies and similar storage are used on the Busiman website, [busiman.org](https://www.busiman.org). For how we handle personal information in general, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Websites use cookies to remember things between visits, such as a login or a language choice.

## Cookies on this website

The Busiman marketing website **does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking pixels**, and it does not follow you across other websites. Because of this, it does not show a cookie banner.

The site may keep small items in your browser that are needed for it to work, such as your chosen theme or a form you are filling in. These stay on your device, are not used to identify you, and are not shared with anyone.

Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure. These are not cookies.

## Busiman apps

The mobile and desktop apps do not use browser cookies. They keep your sign-in in your device's secure storage so you stay logged in. Signing out removes it. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## Third-party content

If a page ever embeds something from another service, such as a video or a payment form, that service may set its own cookies. We will say so on that page. Online payments are completed on the payment provider's own page, under its own policy.

## Managing cookies

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies will not stop the Busiman website from working.

## Changes to this policy

If we add analytics or any other cookies, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
