This page explains how to request that your Busiman account, or your business's whole Busiman account, is deleted.

> **Note:** Busiman is used by businesses to manage their own staff, customers and stock. If you are an employee of a business that uses Busiman, your business admin controls your account and can remove it for you immediately from **Employees** in the app. The steps below are for a business admin requesting deletion of the entire company account, or for anyone who can't reach their admin.

## How to request deletion

Email **[team@busiman.org](mailto:team@busiman.org)** from the address linked to your account, or send the same request from the phone number registered on the account, and include:

- your registered phone number or email address
- your company name (as shown in the app)

We will confirm the request and complete it promptly.

## What gets deleted

- Your account, login details, and session history
- Your attendance, call, sales, purchase, inventory and service report records
- Any fingerprint data enrolled against your account
- Photos, signatures and files you uploaded through the app

## What may be kept, and for how long

- Copies of deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for some time before they are permanently purged, as part of our normal disaster-recovery process.
- Records we are legally required to retain (for example, under tax or labour law) are kept only as long as the law requires, and only for that purpose.

For more detail on what Busiman collects and why, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
