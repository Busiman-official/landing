This policy explains when you can get money back for Busiman. It covers paid plan payments and the prepaid calling wallet. Busiman's core features are free to start, so there is nothing to refund until you pay for something.

> **Note:** This policy applies to payments made to Busiman. Payments your customers make to your business are between you and them, and are not covered here.

## Plan payments

- If you buy a paid plan and it does not work as described, write to us within **7 days of the payment** and we will refund it in full.
- After 7 days, paid plans are not refunded for the period already started. You can cancel at any time and the plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
- If we take a payment by mistake, such as a duplicate or a wrong amount, we will refund it in full whenever you tell us.
- We will tell you the price and billing period before any charge, and we will not charge you for a plan you did not choose.

## The calling wallet

The calling wallet is a separate prepaid balance used for calls on your company number. It is treated apart from plan payments.

- Money added to the wallet is meant to be spent on calls.
- **Unused balance** can be refunded on request, after we remove any call charges already made and any payment gateway fee we could not recover.
- If a wallet top-up fails or is charged twice, we will credit or refund the amount in full.
- Charges for calls already placed are not refunded, because the call provider bills us for them. If a call was billed wrongly, tell us and we will check the record.
- Balance given as a free offer or bonus has no cash value and cannot be refunded.

## How to ask for a refund

1. Write to [team@busiman.org](mailto:team@busiman.org) or call **+91 75006 73358**.
2. Tell us the phone number or company your account uses, the date and amount of the payment, and the payment reference if you have it.
3. We will reply within **3 working days**.

## When refunds arrive

Approved refunds go back to the **same method you paid with** (UPI, card or net banking). The bank or payment provider usually takes **5 to 7 working days** to show it. We cannot speed up the bank's side, but we will send you the refund reference so you can follow it up.

## Cancelling

You can stop using a paid plan at any time. To close your account entirely, follow the steps on the [Delete Your Account](/delete-account) page. Closing an account does not by itself trigger a refund, so ask us first if you want one.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and a change never affects a payment you made before it.
